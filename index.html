How this works: 1 · paste the text  →  2 · press the green button. You get a cleaned copy plus two reports: what was removed, and what only you can judge.

Always removed: zero-width and invisible characters, bidi overrides, variation selectors, control characters. Always flagged for your review: emails, phone numbers, dates, handles, usernames in paths, tokens — the tool cannot know which words identify you. Only you can remove those.

What this checks: EXIF (all fields, decoded), GPS coordinates, camera/lens serials, owner names, XMP edit history, IPTC captions, colour profiles, comments, embedded thumbnails, PNG text chunks, WebP chunks, PDF document fields, Office document properties, container metadata.

What no tool can remove: what is visible or audible in the content itself — faces, signs, screens, voices, distinctive wording. Review content yourself. Saved copies keep real dimensions and file type; a determined examiner can still tell a JPEG is a JPEG.
